GoLog is a simple app for tracking your real trips.
No check-ins, no social feed — just your footprints.

A location is more than a coordinate, and a path is more than a line.
GoLog records your movement so you can look back anytime.

Footprint Map

Shows where you’ve been and the routes you actually took.
Zoom in for details, zoom out to see your overall range.

Trip History

Not only “where”, but also “how often”.
Daily/monthly views help you see your rhythm over time.

Route Replay

Replay any time range and revisit your past trips.
Routes and speeds are shown based on real data.

Data Cleanup

Browse by date, fill gaps, archive, or hide records.
You decide what stays on your map.

City Stays

See which cities you stayed in, for how long, and roughly where you spent time.

Storage

GoLog has no servers and doesn’t collect your data.
Your footprints stay on your device.

If you enable iCloud, data syncs via Apple iCloud and stays in the app’s folder.
You’re in control.
